# Output & Stability Layers

Layers 26–30 form the final stage of Agentic‑30.  
They ensure that all outputs are stable, predictable, aligned with constraints, and safe for delivery.

## Layer 26 — Output Shaping
Applies final formatting, structure, and clarity rules.  
Ensures outputs follow consistent patterns and meet product‑level standards.

## Layer 27 — Stability Enforcement
Monitors the output for volatility, inconsistency, or emergent behaviour.  
Applies stabilising adjustments to maintain predictable system behaviour.

## Layer 28 — Safety Filtering
Runs outputs through the final safety gate.  
Removes unsafe content, enforces compliance, and ensures alignment with constraints.

## Layer 29 — Delivery Validation
Checks that the output is complete, coherent, and ready for delivery.  
Prevents partial, malformed, or contradictory responses from leaving the system.

## Layer 30 — Final Output Layer
Generates the final user‑facing output.  
Guarantees stability, clarity, and alignment with the entire 30‑layer architecture.
