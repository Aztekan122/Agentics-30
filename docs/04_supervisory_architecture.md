# Supervisory Architecture

The supervisory architecture governs Layers 16–20 of Agentic‑30.  
These layers ensure that all reasoning, interpretation, and orchestration remain safe, aligned, and predictable.

## Layer 16 — Drift Detection
Monitors lower‑layer outputs for deviation from constraints, goals, or system principles.  
Flags anomalies for review by higher supervisory layers.

## Layer 17 — Correction & Normalisation
Applies corrective shaping to outputs that show early signs of drift.  
Normalises reasoning patterns to maintain stability.

## Layer 18 — Override Logic
Implements controlled overrides when lower‑layer outputs violate hard constraints or system rules.  
Ensures safety takes precedence over autonomy.

## Layer 19 — Veto Layer
Acts as a final supervisory gate.  
Blocks unsafe, unstable, or misaligned outputs before they reach orchestration.

## Layer 20 — Compliance Enforcement
Ensures all outputs passed to orchestration layers meet system requirements.  
Maintains alignment with global constraints and product‑level objectives.
